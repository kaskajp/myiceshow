## Evidence and limits

The live Skate Canada glossary distinguishes:

- A [three-turn](https://info.skatecanada.ca/index.php/en-ca/other-categories/defined-terms/t.html) changes between outside and inside edges on one foot, turning with the entry curve; its exit continues on the same lobe.
- An [upright position](https://info.skatecanada.ca/index.php/en-ca/other-categories/defined-terms/u.html) uses an extended or slightly bent skating leg and excludes a camel position. [Forward-entry spins](https://info.skatecanada.ca/index.php/en-ca/other-categories/defined-terms/f.html) generally use a forward-outside entry and three-turn into a backward-inside spinning edge.
- A [waltz jump](https://info.skatecanada.ca/index.php/en-ca/other-categories/defined-terms/w.html) takes off forward from an outside edge and makes a half revolution. This glossary entry does not specify the landing foot or edge.

These are terminology checks, not a complete teaching syllabus or proof of an exact foot/direction variant. The proposed left/right choices below are project defaults awaiting review, not quotations from those definitions.

## Gap against the current lab

These are observations of `SkatingMotion.swift`, `SkaterRig.swift`, and `SkaterSkeleton.swift`, not failed claims that the lab is already a skating simulator:

- Every move returns through a shared neutral stance with both ankle targets at ice height. The sampler has no support-foot or edge state; an ankle target alone does not establish blade pressure or contact.
- All root travel follows the same circular path. A three-turn changes facing but does not describe a blade's outside-to-inside edge transfer. Body yaw and direction of travel are separate quantities.
- The spin fixes one blade-center point. It does not model the blade rocker, changing contact patch, or a distinct entry/exit support transfer.
- The jump's root rises for 0.6 seconds, but its limb poses do not encode a reviewed left-foot takeoff and opposite-foot landing. Fixed flight timing is an engineering test parameter, not a measured human jump duration.
- The imported skeleton keeps both ankles level. The lunge's trailing ankle remains at the common height, so its blade/boot orientation and trailing contact cannot yet represent the proposed reviewed lunge.

Do not make these gaps disappear by relabeling the procedural clips as approved variants. Keep the lab as the comparison baseline while evaluating an authored candidate.

## Clip handoff requirements

For each reviewed candidate, record these alongside the asset, before changing the runtime animation path:

1. **Evidence:** source/publisher, exact segment start/end, what was actually observed, named skating reviewer/date, corrections, and separately documented asset/animation rights. A tutorial being publicly viewable is not a reuse license.
2. **Coordinates:** meters, axes/handedness, neutral pose, blade geometry and local contact frame, skeleton hierarchy, and any conversion into the lab's Y-up/+Z-forward convention.
3. **Phases:** entry, execution, exit, and the landmark times separating them. Specify support foot, edge, heading, travel tangent, and free-foot clearance for each phase. Record contact intervals rather than inferring them from zero root height.
4. **Timing:** which preparation/exit portions may lengthen, which performance portions must remain fixed, and the supported duration range. The lab's jump interval and three-revolution spin are not production authoring mandates.
5. **Root ownership:** whether the clip contains root motion or is in-place, who applies world placement, and how the player samples at absolute show time. Do not apply baked root travel twice or start a second independently accumulating playback clock.
6. **Garments:** outfit/skin checks at the lowest pose, free-leg lift, spin, takeoff, and landing, including self-intersections and blade/ice penetration.

This is a review checklist, not a speculative application schema or a new metadata framework.

## Acceptance at the next runtime spike

These are proposed checks to run with real clips; none has passed for an authored animation yet:

- Compare an isolated clip with its signed-off example at entry, execution landmarks, and exit. Judge both feet and the blade path, not just the pelvis or silhouette.
- Sample the same absolute time while playing, paused, seeking backward, and after restarting. Observe the same pose, placement, contacts, and music position.
- Assess all 25 ordered move pairs, including repeats. Record any required connecting step or support transfer; reject impossible direct blends rather than hiding them with slower interpolation. Clips may require distinct entry/exit states instead of the lab's common neutral pose.
- Assess permitted minimum/default/maximum durations without stretching airborne motion or sliding a supporting foot. Set tolerances with the reviewer and actual asset scale; the mathematical fixture tolerances are not production contact criteria.
- Measure frame time, memory, and sustained playback on a chosen physical iPhone and iPad. Simulator success is not this gate.
